# Showcase assets

The current Lite landing uses the user's revised colorful optical direction,
grounded in live reference-site captures. Its decorative assets do not supply
financial evidence. The grayscale paper asset remains available for older views.

## Paper sculpture

- File: `paper-sculpture.webp`.
- Dimensions: 1400 × 1004 pixels; 124,226 bytes.
- Origin: an original image generated for Prispect from the selected grayscale
  concept, showing five curled report-paper sheets on a transparent background.
- Processing: cropped to the alpha bounds with small padding, converted to
  neutral grayscale, resized and encoded as WebP while preserving transparency.
- Use: decorative hero imagery only. Its indistinct print and chart marks are
  illustration, not company records, financial values or source excerpts.

## Optical prism

- File: `optical-prism.webp`.
- Dimensions: 1254 × 1254 pixels; 420,898 bytes, with alpha transparency.
- Origin: an original image generated for Prispect using the built-in Image Gen
  tool on 2026-10-04. The source file is
  `/workspace/generated_images/exec-c78e3693-8da1-4e1b-9e4c-2236e499f706.png`.
- Art direction: a sculptural black-chrome optical ring containing twisted
  translucent crystal-glass slabs, with acid-lime edge lighting and cyan/violet
  iridescence. No third-party website asset, logo, photograph or financial chart
  was copied into this image.
- Processing: encoded from the generated PNG as WebP using Sharp, quality 90,
  alpha quality 100, without color changes or cropping.
- Rights/provenance: generated for this project; no third-party stock license
  or attribution requirement is asserted. The source and generation record
  remain separate from the original financial-report excerpts.
- Use: decorative hero imagery only; it contains no financial values, company
  records or source evidence.

## Display font

- File: `display.woff`; 23,044 bytes.
- Source: Noto Sans CJK SC Bold, face 2 of the installed
  `/usr/share/fonts/opentype/noto/NotoSansCJK-Bold.ttc` collection.
- Upstream: <https://github.com/notofonts/noto-cjk>.
- Derivation: fontTools subset containing the landing and menu headline
  characters and Latin characters; converted to WOFF 1. The modified family is
  `Prispect Display`, and its PostScript name is `PrispectDisplay-Bold`.
- License: SIL Open Font License 1.1, retained in
  [FONT-LICENSE.txt](FONT-LICENSE.txt). The upstream Adobe copyright embedded in
  the source font remains in the subset; the accompanying notice also retains
  the Google attribution from the installed package's font copyright record.

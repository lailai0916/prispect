Safety Systems Co., Ltd.'s **2025 annual report**, published on 2026-04-11.
They support a fixed historical example in the Prispect homepage.

The source text, layout, comparative column, signs and numbers remain unchanged.
The PNGs are table excerpts, not complete pages or translated reproductions. Page,
issuer, reporting period and original link must remain visible with them.

The homepage's **other disclosed adjustments** amount, CNY 217,235,539.32,
independently sums 13 retained original rows across both pages. It is not the
original table's single “Other” row, CNY 3,784,813.90. The other grouped amount,
three working-capital adjustments and profit reconcile exactly to CNY 26,197,123.70
of operating cash flow. This equality does not establish a business cause.

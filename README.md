## Website Introduction

Company search, charts and company questions work without signing in. Each visitor receives a separate browser research workspace, and registration is open by default. Private materials, payment plans and account settings still require an account; visitor research is not automatically moved into one.

Lite at `/` provides a search-first ordinary-user experience: financial, public-record, risk and reputation prompts, varied local motion and real original-page interactions. Its saved-company dossier connects each finding to its exact metric and recorded sources, with reverse citations and local source pages. Pro at `/query` retains the professional seven-page workspace. Switching experiences preserves the same owning record without starting research. Both provide complete printable report content. See the [eight software references and design choices](docs/software-ui-reference-review.md).

Prispect (析光) starts from a company or a matter to investigate. Company queries first show structured annual financials, operating cash and source-linked rule observations. After the public snapshot is saved, configured AI research runs independently in the background; readers can continue browsing data and open the completed analysis. Explicit deep research and original-report review remain available. External users can continue into a prepayment review; incoming operators can examine a dated cash plan. Private records support their own conditional calculations. Historical financial signals never fill current cash or future receipts.

Current company research supports A-share issuers. Reading defaults to profit attributable to owners and may switch to consolidated profit; financial screening and original cash bridges retain annual consolidated CNY evidence. New US-market research is paused; account-owned historical records and retained originals remain accessible. Unsupported currencies retain their original units without conversion or CNY reinterpretation.

Banks, securities firms and insurers are included in public financial retrieval and analysis. Income metadata selects the corresponding cash-flow and balance-sheet schemas. All supported issuers use the same metrics, thresholds, grading and report presentation; requested model analysis follows the same rules. Available amounts, ratios and evidence remain visible; absent fields are not replaced with zeros. See [financial-institution support](docs/financial-institution-support-2026-10-03.md).

Built for the Xuejun High School “Echo · 48H Youth Creation Camp”, X-Ray direction. The original cash bridge answers a narrow financial question; the company research Agent adds broader public-data analysis. A single historical cash ratio is not an overall grade. Prispect's transparent financial screening grade is not a credit-agency rating, credit decision or investment advice. Customer demand and willingness to pay remain research assumptions.

The [documentation hub](https://prispect.com/docs) links to six articles: [About Prispect](https://prispect.com/docs/about), [User guide](https://prispect.com/docs/guide), [Review methodology](https://prispect.com/docs/methodology), [Privacy policy](https://prispect.com/docs/privacy), [Terms of service](https://prispect.com/docs/terms) and [Copyright notice](https://prispect.com/docs/copyright). Previous article URLs and guide section links remain compatible. Policies describe the implemented service rather than certifying legal compliance. Contact: `lailai0x394@gmail.com`.

Validation uses the full local check and browser interaction replays; provider checks and production release verification are tracked separately. For the current Lite/Pro change, see [its verification state](docs/lite-pro.md#verification), which remains separate from earlier acceptance records. See [the reconstruction plan and acceptance tasks](docs/rebuild-plan.md) and [production capacity record](docs/production-capacity-2026-10-03.md) for scope, evidence and release readiness.

The [version-pinned competitive engineering record](docs/competitive-research/README.md) contains five repository studies, the complete value inventory, design alternatives, implementation mapping and before/after acceptance. Its evidence distinguishes live requests, cached results, synthetic fixtures and unavailable providers. These mechanisms do not authenticate documents, approve payment or turn source counts into confidence.

## Website Features

💾 **Saved Company Research** — Repeated selection of the same company, annual year, purpose and research mode opens the owner's existing research without new retrieval or model work. Up to twelve public snapshots render from bounded browser storage across reloads, retaining their original dates until an explicit refresh. Private originals, questions and user goals are excluded; deletion, reset, logout and account changes clear the corresponding local copies. Server-side resource reuse also shares validated anonymous responses, content-addressed official PDF parsing and complete same-year peer cohorts. Successful assistant answers reuse only an exact owning-account question, conversation and evidence version; further research and explicit refresh bypass saved answers. Three explanation challenges retain separate source-bound results with a Research again action. See [resource-cache behavior and verification](docs/resource-cache-2026-10-03.md).

🔍 **Evidence Review and Action Feedback** — Evidence drawers connect stored excerpts to their supported fields and separate calculated metrics from quoted values. Selected financial fields expose both recorded source amounts, scope and differences in the same drawer. Payment exposure has an on-demand explanation using the current branch's amounts, and editing a saved item marks meaningful changes as pending recalculation. Research in progress or after failure retains links to acquired data and its actual dates; cancelling a specific public-research revision preserves sources and reports, and late callbacks cannot overwrite a retry. Assistant suggestions fill an editable draft, while dependency practice distinguishes missed and extra predictions and exposes actual dependency paths. These enhance existing flows; universal PDF sentence localization and account-wide material full-text search are not implemented.

🔐 **Personal Accounts** — Better Auth registration, avatar and profile settings, password-strength checks, TOTP, one-use recovery codes, passkeys and session management. Materials, reviews and exports are scoped to their owner. Mail verification and recovery require SMTP; absent providers return unavailable.

🏢 **Pro Company Workspace and Navigation** — Pro starts at `/query` and retains the professional report and sidebar. Seven fixed entries appear directly below New research: Research report, Financial trends, Industry comparison, Disclosure leads, Extended checks, Data coverage and Source comparison. The menu has no company-group heading; the unified assistant remains in the lower-right corner. Navigation uses the current owning-account company or the most recently created query, rather than the most recently updated record. With no record, all seven entries are disabled; start from New research. The New research page always shows the logo and a centered company input. Use Loaded companies in the sidebar to open the account's saved records without starting new research; records and caches are retained. Legacy `/research` links redirect to `/query`. Saved company, original-report and private-workflow links remain compatible.

📄 **Research report** — The Research report page immediately presents annual profit, operating cash, liquidity and rule observations with exact calculations and source drawers. Its independent AI view shows real research stages. New financial AI reports contain a 100–200-character Chinese core paragraph, validated source-bound highlights and at least two distinct generated follow-up questions; cached older reports remain readable. Report questions open and submit to the unified assistant in the same company/year and displayed profit-basis context. Saved data and the previous dated report remain readable during research or failure; revisits do not start another analysis. Reading defaults to attributable profit, with consolidated profit available from the same control. Financial trends, industry comparison, announcements, extended checks, coverage and source comparison have separate pages. Existing deep judgments, financial grades, evidence labs and original confirmation remain available on demand. Web fields remain separate from adopted original evidence.

📍 **Reading Continuity** — The report’s follow-up count opens its existing check list, with each item using the retained evidence entry. The company-page index marks the current chapter. Returning after a source or financial review resumes reading within the same account, year and source/report snapshots; new scopes reset and explicit section links take priority. Reading state stays in page memory and does not change reports, grades or evidence.

🔎 **Focused Research and Follow-ups** — Requested deep research retains relevant passages and contrary clues within existing budgets, with explicit omissions and duplicate references. Private questions bind replies and originals to immutable versions; replies remain unverified statements. The [bounded model comparison](docs/research-depth-live-check.md) distinguishes synthetic inputs from real calls.

📰 **Source Reader** — Acquired news and public discussions retain local keyword, media and content-scope filters, date sorting and pagination, with the selected source text available on narrow screens. Titles, digests and retrieved excerpts remain distinct. Filtering saved sources makes no model or public-source request.

🧭 **Research Entry and Questions** — The entry preserves the current owner's draft through navigation and confirms a matched issuer before starting saved research. Public company codes and short names match a preloaded directory; misses use bounded live search. Enter starts matching immediately, and ambiguous results retain a selection step. Only Lite’s lower-right assistant launcher uses the user-referenced mouthless Grok Bot ball, with white hair, blue eyes, pink cheeks and a blue-green hair ornament. Its fixed-position companion gently sways, blinks and bounces while chat is closed. Idle motion pauses on hover or keyboard focus and while the page is hidden. Clicking opens chat immediately with happy closed eyes and a gentle bounce; reduced motion keeps it still. Expanded chat cards contain no character avatars. Pro and other pages use the professional round chat icon. Switching Lite/Pro preserves the same conversation and draft. It has one conversation for researched companies, product help and policy details. It resolves company context automatically, follows up with bounded public retrieval when needed, and cites actual company sources or canonical product documents. Visitor product questions search documents locally; company questions use only that visitor’s public research and may call the model. Account questions use the configured model. Failed calls retain truthful rule answers or document excerpts.

🗂️ **Original-Report Compatibility** — Requested original review retains the LangGraph workflow for official A-share annual reports, relevant notes and selected announcement originals. Rules extract amounts and preserve missing fields and conflicts; reviewed candidates become personal materials only after adoption. Default financial acquisition does not start this workflow or download PDFs; subsequent public AI research may use its separate bounded official-excerpt tool. See the [real-company algorithm regression record](docs/algorithm-verification-2026-10-02.md).

📊 **Financial trends and Industry** — Structured Eastmoney financial interfaces retain up to six annual periods. Four thematic groups each contain four metric cards, with an optional All charts view; bars, lines and debt-component dumbbells share the neutral chart system. Profit and operating cash share an amount scale, while each card expands exact annual values and sources. Financial trends and Industry comparison share the selected year; selecting a year or presentation makes no request. Opening either chart page fills only unsettled missing peer cohorts sequentially, and reopening reuses saved cohorts and failures. Explicit refresh bypasses shared caches; failures retain the previous dated data. Industry switches among comparison charts, distributions and data details, with each card opening its same-year histogram. Missing or conflicting fields stay unknown and web fields are never adopted automatically. See the [F mainline record](docs/f-core-mainline-2026-10-03.md).

🧮 **Reproducible Financial Analysis** — JSON, CSV and text-PDF preview; editable confirmation; period, currency and scope checks; exact integer-fen computation; persisted reports and questions. Two-period profit and operating-cash changes retain source references for both periods and exact formulas. Percentage growth requires a positive prior-year base; conflicts withhold comparisons. Original-report input or currency conflicts mean evidence needs review and pause dependent interpretation; they do not establish company risk or a company rating. Public news and discussion analysis stays in the existing company research record, without a separate reputation RSS request when opening an original report.

🔎 **Evidence and Decision Versions** — A sourced cash bridge, page references and competing explanations lead to evidence requests. Combined rules save whether their conditions hold, do not all hold or cannot yet be evaluated, with dependencies and source-backed gaps. Each combined signal offers a single-dependency review and a copyable evidence request for an external payer or incoming operator. Payment decisions retain input/evidence versions, source-text bindings and known conflicts. Withdrawing direct evidence withholds dependent record calculations while keeping unrelated facts and explicitly entered assumptions.

🧪 **Payment Scenarios** — External scenarios compare undelivered exposure after proposed payments against a user-set limit. Internal scenarios compare specific payment dates, event balances, period ends and conditional payment/collection thresholds across listed 90-day events. Same-day order, coverage and negotiation assumptions remain visible. Missing inputs remain unknown.

🌐 **Bilingual Workflow** — Chinese and English product flows, documentation and policies. The language control shows the current language; appearance follows the system on page load and system changes, with a one-click light/dark toggle available anytime. Materials, cash plans and avatars accept drag-and-drop with validation and retry. Reports expose payment and handover priorities with sourced, ordered evidence requests and suggested contacts. HTML, JSON and Markdown checklists are previewed before saving; the saved content is the previewed snapshot. Original source quotations retain their language.

⌨️ **Workspace Details** — Header search and ⌘ K / Ctrl K jump to pages, saved companies, reports and materials in the current account. List filters show counts; searches clear with Escape, reviews can be sorted, and material results open at the matched record. Shared controls, empty states, loading placeholders and brief transitions respect reduced motion. Page modules preload on link hover or keyboard focus; navigation keeps the outgoing content visible until the destination commits. Research counts and inputs keep their positions while records load, and language changes do not clear acquired records. Original-document review reuses the selected record already read by the workspace; loading never flashes a new-query form or a second history hint, and module loading uses an evidence-shaped placeholder. Chart tabs and window resizing keep plot heights stable; language changes preserve ongoing peer retrieval, and snapshot refreshes do not repeat completed section navigation. Back and forward restore each visit's reading position after content arrives, yielding to user scrolling. Same-research public-source updates retain filters, the selected article and reader scroll; active-tab clicks preserve selection, and removed sources close their drawer with focus returned to the list. A workspace refresh failure retains loaded content and offers a read-only retry only after confirming the same account; owner changes and expired sessions still hide the old workspace. Success notices pause on hover, focus or hidden tabs; errors remain until dismissed. Export previews confirm download initiation without claiming a file was saved to disk.

🧠 **Background AI Research** — After successful structured acquisition, a server queue starts bounded public financial, news, discussion and official-excerpt research when the model is configured. Explicit deep research remains available. Actual stages, source coverage, failures and excerpt limits remain visible; no simulated delay or completion is added. The model cannot change the financial grade. Failed same-snapshot work needs an explicit retry, and unconfigured automatic analysis makes no additional search. The acquisition and research workflows remain independent. See [the workflow and method](docs/company-research-agent.md).

💹 **Company Identity and Market Snapshot** — Issuer identity, business details and an attempted Eastmoney quote remain available without duplicating the report's judgment. Prices, changes, market cap and source quote time retain source precision. Missing or blocked fields stay unavailable; a retrieved snapshot is not guaranteed to be real-time and does not alter the selected-year financial grade.

📐 **Transparent Analysis Grade** — Selected-year consolidated profitability, cash quality, solvency and working capital have equal weights. Weak dimensions cap the overall grade; missing or conflicting required inputs leave the complete grade as NR. Independently supported dimensions may produce a read-only provisional grade using their observed-score mean and the same caps, with explicit coverage and no changes to the saved complete grade. Peer and event analysis provide separate context. Formulas and sources remain inspectable in the report, with full grading criteria in the methodology document; a headline alone cannot establish an adverse event.

🔬 **Evidence Lab** — Inspect the links between source facts, calculations, competing explanations and requested materials. Temporarily withdraw or restore a fact to pause only its dependent paths. Company web snapshots use year-end balance changes. Trials run locally without model calls or changes to saved evidence, reports or grades. See [the method and walkthrough](docs/evidence-lab.md).

🧭 **Challenge an Explanation** — For a saved company, examine expansion stocking, inventory sell-through pressure or collection pressure through actual targeted public searches and bounded official-PDF reads. The configured model organizes supporting and counter clues with source links and clearly unobtained distinguishing materials. Missing configuration still permits the fixed public research steps and rule clues; failures do not become invented results or confidence scores. Local withdrawal state and private working papers are excluded from challenge inputs.

🔎 **Lite and Pro Experiences** — Lite at `/` uses a colorful optical scene with kinetic type, an iridescent prism, local light orbits, scroll transitions and a draggable reveal of real historical report excerpts. Its real company search opens a separate guided result at `/company?run=…&experience=lite`, organized into summary, figures, sources and questions. Pro at `/query` retains the professional report and seven data pages. Both share company matching, annual selection, accounts, saved evidence and calculations; switching on a company retains the same saved run without new research or source/model calls. Report questions preserve the company, year, displayed profit basis and saved report generation. Motion respects reduced motion and does not alter figures or execution state. The original evidence story remains at `/?view=story`. See [Lite/Pro architecture and verification](docs/lite-pro.md) and [the retained introduction](docs/cinematic-home.md).

## Getting Started

Use Node.js 22.12 or newer and npm. Access to this private repository requires authorization.

```bash
git clone https://github.com/lailai0916/prispect.git
cd prispect
npm ci
npm run build
npm start
```

Open `http://localhost:4317` and start company research without signing in. For private materials and plans, register with a hard-to-guess password of 8–128 characters. New accounts start with an empty workspace. On macOS, `start.command` performs the installation if needed, builds and starts the application. For development use `npm run dev` and open `http://localhost:4318`. Set `APP_ORIGIN` to the actual browser origin when using passkeys on a different port; production requires HTTPS and a persistent `BETTER_AUTH_SECRET`.

```bash
npm run check
npm run data:fetch
npm run data:samples
```

`check` runs strict types, financial/account/API tests, the client build and formatting checks. `data:fetch` downloads only manifest-listed public reports and verifies SHA256; the original PDFs are excluded from Git. `data:samples` prepares structured import examples. Scanned PDFs and ambiguous table values require confirmation rather than invented extraction.

Financial amounts and calculations use rules. New company queries default to `researchMode=financial`, with official issuer confirmation and structured public interfaces. This acquisition stage makes no model call or PDF download; a configured server starts independent public AI analysis only after saving a stable snapshot. Source refresh creates a new analysis generation after acquisition succeeds, while GETs, cache reads and revisits reuse saved state. See [background analysis and verification scope](docs/company-auto-analysis-2026-10-03.md). Explicit `deep` research, company assistant questions and financial-report explanations retain their configured server-side model workflow; copies, retries and resumes preserve the mode, and legacy records remain compatible. Missing configuration, conflicting evidence or failed calls retain truthful rule results. Model configuration belongs in the server `.env`; see the privacy policy for payload scope. Public research uses allowlisted evidence; complete uploads, private review descriptions, notes and cash plans are excluded from company-model payloads. Signed-out document help makes no model calls. Private CSV/JSON plans remain draft conditions, and citation/numeric checks do not establish semantic correctness. Public-source failures retain their actual coverage.

Accounts, sessions and each user's working papers persist in `CASHLENS_DATA_DIR` (default `.cashlens`). Preserve the entire directory and authentication secret; read [deployment and backup notes](docs/deployment.md) before deployment or recovery. The public product domain is [prispect.com](https://prispect.com). Domain migration and live verification are tracked separately from historical releases. Release checks and verification boundaries are recorded in [the acceptance report](docs/acceptance.md). This is a single-process product without measured production capacity or multi-node availability. SMTP remains unconfigured and the interface reports unavailable email verification. A display phone number can be edited or cleared in Profile without SMS verification; it is not used for sign-in or password recovery.

Main-branch CI gates deployment through a restricted SSH entry, with artifact/source integrity checks, a consistent state backup and asset verification. New packages retain hashed frontend assets from the three nearest successful main-push CI ancestors so browsers opened before an update can still load those pages. Entry HTML stays current; clients outside that window or after a rollback may need to reload. Rollback is allowed only when the previous release can read the current authentication schema. See [automatic deployment](docs/actions-deployment.md).

See [the plan](docs/plan.md), [method and source research](docs/research.md), [API contract](docs/api.md), [data lineage](docs/data-lineage.md), [reference proposal review](docs/reference-data-review.md), [AI-use disclosure](docs/ai-usage.md) and [competition submission requirements](docs/submission-checklist.md). Private-repository badges may be unavailable.

A repeatable local walkthrough is available after `npm run build`: run `node --import tsx scripts/serve-research-demo.ts`, open the printed loopback URL, create a local demo account and search `601234`. It uses clearly labeled synthetic company data, a fresh temporary workspace and rule analysis; it does not query public sources or call a model. Existing nonempty workspaces and production mode are rejected. See [the two walkthroughs and validation boundaries](docs/research-flow-acceptance-2026-10-03.md).

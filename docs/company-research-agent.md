# Company research and analysis

The company workspace combines bounded public research with a reproducible financial screen. The configured OpenAI-compatible model (default `grok-4.7-fast`) plans additional reads and writes attributed judgments. It cannot change financial values, the screening method or the resulting grade. This document describes the implementation; it does not claim that a live Grok deployment has been independently verified.

## Flow and scope

1. Resolve a supported Shanghai/Shenzhen listed issuer and retrieve its public company snapshot. Automatic analysis starts after context retrieval; an account owner can also start or repeat research through `POST /api/company-runs/:id/assessment`.
2. Run the company research agent on a public copy. Normal company research first collects a broader news and public-discussion sample and attempts a market snapshot. The configured model inspects the actual coverage, requests supported follow-ups and decides whether more source checks are useful.
3. Derive the selected-year consolidated financial screen on the enriched copy. A separate model phase produces a validated bilingual summary, six dimension judgments, strengths, risks, actions and conditions that would improve or weaken the judgment. When broader public signals are present, another model request reviews counterarguments and source attribution before final publication.
4. Publish only if the account-owned record, assessment revision and public snapshot still match. Refreshes or deletion invalidate an older job. Progress records contain actual start/finish events, outcomes and errors; they do not simulate percentage completion.

An optional research focus is an explicit instruction sent to the model, limited to 1,000 characters. It changes the research emphasis, not the company, accounting period or screening method. The agent is company-scoped rather than a general browser or code executor.

## Actual tools and budgets

| Tool                     | Behavior                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| ------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `collect_public_signals` | Once per normal company research job, retrieves bounded Eastmoney/Sina news catalogs and the issuer's public Eastmoney Guba board, with a few actual body reads. Retains at most 180 deduplicated news records and 240 discussion records.                        |
| `get_market_quote`       | Attempts one Eastmoney public market request per job. Confirms the returned security code, preserves exact price/change/market-cap strings and the source quote timestamp, and reports partial or unavailable fields. Does not alter historical financial scores. |
| `get_financial_history`  | Reads existing public annual history, computed metrics and provenance; does not adopt original-report candidates.                                                                                                                                                 |
| `fetch_industry`         | Retrieves the selected annual period's same-industry peers, at most once per job; can reuse a complete matching snapshot less than 24 hours old.                                                                                                                  |
| `search_disclosures`     | Searches the company's acquired announcement archive, capped at 1,200 records, returning at most 12 matching IDs. This searches the acquired archive.                                                                                                             |
| `search_news`            | Queries Eastmoney with the confirmed company name and a validated topic. Reads up to three actual pages of 30 results, checks issuer names/codes and dates, then merges into the 180-record news limit.                                                           |
| `search_discussions`     | Searches the acquired public-discussion catalog by topic and returns up to 20 matches. This does not query another social network or obtain new posts.                                                                                                            |
| `read_news`              | Accepts a known news ID whose URL matches a supported fixed Eastmoney Finance or Sina Finance article path. Reads at most 1.5 MB and retains up to 4,000 text characters with the response hash and read time.                                                    |
| `read_discussion`        | Accepts a known issuer-board post ID. Checks the body response's issuer and post ID and retains up to 4,000 text characters. Public poster statements remain unverified opinions; comments, images, videos and account profiles are not collected.                |
| `read_disclosure`        | Accepts a known company announcement ID, never a supplied URL. Reads an allowed official PDF and preserves a page excerpt and file hash.                                                                                                                          |

The research phase allows **six model-planning turns, 24 tool attempts, six new PDF reads and 300 seconds**. Its shared supplementary reader permits **72 public requests** across collection, market, news, post and PDF reads. Each planning request has a 60-second deadline. These are ceilings, not a promised minimum duration or number of reads. In normal broad research, a first planning response that immediately stops receives a second review opportunity to consider remaining questions and contrary evidence. This makes an actual model request, with no artificial waiting time or claim to expose the model's private reasoning. Each PDF is limited to 8 MB, has a 45-second read deadline, and contributes only a selected excerpt from its first three pages, up to 1,500 characters. Previously read excerpts can be reused.

The automatic collector has a further **26-request ceiling within that shared budget**: up to six Eastmoney pages of 30 news results, one Sina company-news catalog, three Guba board pages of 80 posts, eight news-body attempts and eight post-body attempts. Body selection starts with a recent item and then considers financial/operating relevance, topic variety, media labels and dates. It does not manufacture a balanced positive/negative opinion distribution. Reported totals, short pages, source failures, cancellation or budgets can reduce actual coverage. News from both catalogs is filtered and deduplicated before the 180-record retention cap. A catalog entry does not mean the body was read. Optional market retrieval uses one additional shared request, capped at 2 MB; unavailable access preserves unknown values and an error receipt. Quote time comes only from the source field, and may be the previous trading day. Retrieval time does not establish real-time pricing.

Automatic collection and market retrieval each count as a tool attempt. Server-owned `initialCalls` can also execute supported, validated tools before model planning, sharing the same 24-tool / 72-request limits. Explanation challenges use this path for deterministic financial, disclosure and news checks, plus up to two matching official-PDF reads. Actual public tools can run without a model key; the planning step then explicitly records that AI is unconfigured. See [the evidence lab and challenge method](evidence-lab.md).

Industry retrieval has its own bounded pagination and source-reader budget, subject to the research phase's cancellation signal; it is not included in the 72 supplementary requests. Initial context retrieval separately has a 90-second / 50-request budget. Final synthesis has a separate **180-second phase deadline, 90 seconds per request and at most three requests**: initial analysis, one eligible format/citation repair if needed, and the separate counterargument review for broadened public research. If that review fails, the first validated draft is retained with a warning; an invalid initial draft cannot become an accepted report. Thus the research and synthesis ceilings total 480 seconds, excluding separate initial context and industry limits. This is not an end-to-end latency promise or a required wait.

These tools use configured public A-share sources and fixed allowed hosts. They do not implement Google/Bing whole-web search, X search, or native Grok browser tools. Compatibility with a model endpoint does not establish support for a provider's native web-search features.

## News and public-discussion coverage

Public signal coverage records raw results, accepted records, retained unique records, actual pages, reported totals when supplied, successful automatic body reads, oldest/latest dates and the stop reason. Source receipts keep each actual read's status, retrieval time and response hash. A reported search total is neither the retained company sample nor proof that every result is relevant. Failures and unknown totals remain visible.

Each public model payload contains source-linked titles, dates, media/provider labels and available text. News and discussion text share a **140,000-character ceiling**; repeated clusters/text appear once within that payload, with references to the earlier copy. Truncation, omitted text, excerpt counts and excluded records are reported explicitly. A further serialized-input size guard can shorten text. The full retained source catalog remains available for citation validation and UI inspection; sending a title or shortened excerpt never implies reading a complete article or all posts.

Eastmoney and Sina may distribute another publisher's report or a company-authored item. An aggregator is not necessarily the originating media; reposts and repeated opinions are not independent corroboration. Read media text remains a news lead, with its separate `media-excerpt` scope. Guba text has `opinion` quality whether only a title or a body excerpt was obtained. It is a sample from one public issuer board, not verified company evidence or a representative public-opinion survey. Neither article/post counts nor sentiment change the deterministic grade or become a confidence percentage. Professional synthesis should weigh supporting and contrary clues alongside financial and official-disclosure evidence.

## Data quality and isolation

Public annual history retains up to six periods. The screen fixes the requested full annual period and consolidated CNY basis; later annual/interim figures cannot replace it. Three-year context requires the selected year and its two immediately preceding years. Later news and announcements retain their own observation dates.

Wrong issuers, inconsistent duplicate rows, cross-source conflicts and unavailable fields withhold their dependent metrics. Amounts need public provenance; failed table receipts cannot be bypassed with retained values. Unsupported issuers and financial-sector businesses receive `NR`. Industry comparisons require matching issuer/year, complete same-year pagination and at least five valid peers per metric, excluding the target.

Evidence distinguishes third-party web fields, read official-disclosure excerpts, media leads and public opinions. A title, news digest, public post, empty search or retrieval failure does not establish default, misconduct or absence of risk. Strong event assertions require matching actual disclosure text and the correct actor: an issuer suing a defaulting counterparty does not establish issuer default. Allegations and unresolved disputes remain attributed. A response/file hash records retrieval integrity, not authenticity or the correctness of an interpretation. Historical monetary funds are not current available cash; operating cash is not sales receipts.

Model payloads are rebuilt from public allowlists. They exclude account identifiers, uploaded candidate previews, private working papers, payment decisions, cash plans and internal notes. Research supplements remain public snapshots and never become adopted original-report evidence automatically. Tools cannot change the issuer or accept arbitrary URLs. Model output must reference existing evidence/available metrics; numeric statements use server-rendered metric placeholders. Citation checks do not replace substantive review of an interpretation.

## Transparent financial screen

`financial-screen-v1` assigns equal 25% weights to four core dimensions. Amount calculations use integer cents; thresholds compare unrounded fractions. Display rounding does not determine scores.

| Dimension                | Screening rule                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| ------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Profitability and growth | Positive profit with nondeclining revenue: 100; revenue decline up to 10%: 60; larger decline: 25. Zero profit: 25; loss: 0. Growth requires positive prior revenue.                                                                                                                                        |
| Operating cash quality   | With positive profit, cash/profit at least 100%: 100; at least 70%: 60; below 70%: 25; negative cash: 0. With nonpositive profit, the ratio is inapplicable: positive/zero/negative cash scores 60/25/0.                                                                                                    |
| Solvency and leverage    | Cash covering the two named short-debt fields at least 1x/0.5x/below 0.5x scores 100/60/25. Liabilities/assets at most 50%/70%/90%/above 90% scores 100/60/25/0. These signals are equally weighted; zero debt in both fields uses leverage alone. Other obligations and restricted funds are not resolved. |
| Working-capital pressure | Compare `(receivables + inventory) / revenue` with the prior year. A nonincrease scores 100; an increase up to 10 percentage points scores 60; a larger increase scores 25. This is not a bad-debt rate or cash-conversion cycle.                                                                           |

All four dimensions require their critical data without conflicts. Missing weights are never redistributed. A missing required field, invalid core denominator or excluded scope gives `NR` and a null aggregate score.

The arithmetic score initially maps to A ≥ 80, B ≥ 60, C ≥ 40, otherwise D. **One core dimension below 40 caps the final grade at C; two or more cap it at D.** The original arithmetic score remains visible, with `ratingConstraints` explaining the cap. Thus an 81.25 arithmetic score with a cash score of 25 receives C rather than A. These are transparent, uncalibrated screening rules, not official or credit-agency grades.

Industry position and events/governance complete the six analysis dimensions. They provide evidence-backed qualitative context without count-based penalties or automatic additions to the financial score. Grok can explain competing signals and priorities but cannot override the final grade.

## Fallback, caching and accounting

Unconfigured, failed or invalid model results preserve available public data and the deterministic screen. Unconfigured normal research can still perform automatic public collection, attempt a market snapshot, run provided `initialCalls` and attempt a bounded industry read; it does not claim AI planning occurred. Failed tools retain unknown states and truthful trace entries. Older saved results are not silently rewritten.

`model.calls` counts final-analysis request attempts, including failed, repair and counterargument-review calls; `research.modelCalls` includes planning and synthesis attempts. `toolCalls` records attempted executions, including failed tools. Missing model configuration produces zero external model calls. A successful report alone is not evidence that all sources or calls succeeded.

Manual assessment starts/restarts are limited to **12 per hour per account**. Matching ready results are reused before charging this limiter. The assessment cache key includes issuer, selected year, snapshot time/revision, selected-year industry snapshot and research focus. A refresh or changed key requests new research; the cache does not relabel old observation dates as current. Concurrent starts reuse the existing job, while changing its focus requires that job to finish first.
